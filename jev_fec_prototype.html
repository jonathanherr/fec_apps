FEC × Jev prototype: 2024 head-to-head spending & donors

Trump vehicle C00828541 (NEVER SURRENDER, INC. — $495M receipts 2024) vs Harris vehicle C00703975 (HARRIS VICTORY FUND — $1.17B receipts 2024). FEC API supplies facts; Jev jev-1.13.0 supplies semantic normalization (Choice).

1. Spending mix by Jev-normalized tactic (Schedule B, 2024 activity only)

60+ distinct free-text disbursement_description values per committee → 7 canonical tactics via batched Jev Choice calls. Dollars summed in code, never by the model. Both sides: cursor walk down the amount curve (Trump 10 pages × 100, Harris 12 × 100), 2024 activity only. Trump: 544 distinct records, $408.2M (87% of $471.5M totals). Harris: 630 records, $1,008.8M (86% of $1,175.2M totals).

Method notes. (1) The FEC API ignores page when sort is set — pages 1–5 returned identical sub_ids. Fixed by deduping on sub_id and cursoring with last_disbursement_amount + last_index from each response's pagination.last_indexes. Unsorted paging is not an alternative — it 504s on large committees. (2) cycle=2024 still returns 2020-dated rows for C00703975, so all figures filter on disbursement_date in 2024 and reconcile against /committee/{id}/totals/?cycle=2024. (3) Top-by-amount sampling hides the mix on both sides: Harris's top-100 is 100% media; Trump's top-100 showed $0.96M legal vs $10.5M in the full walk (152 small lawyer payments). Amount-ranked samples systematically hide high-frequency / low-amount tactics.

2. Donor occupation sectors (Schedule A itemized individuals, n=100 sample each)

16 distinct contributor_occupation strings → 9 sectors via one batched Jev Choice call (16 parallel questions, conf 0.99–1.00).
